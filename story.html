The Girl with the Blue Wallet

That evening, I went for my usual evening walk in the state park. While walking, I noticed a diary and an unused pen lying on a remote bench. I knew it was wrong to read someone's private diary, but out of curiosity, I opened it. The diary belonged to an old lady. She had been writing in it for the past two years. I sat there to wait for its owner. After some time, when no one came, I put it back and went for my walk.

On my way back, I saw a girl looking around anxiously. She came towards me and asked if I had seen a diary. She told me that it belonged to her grandma, who had died that very day. I noticed that she was carrying a small, blue, leather wallet. I asked to see it to check if she had any ID or contact information , but it was completely empty.

I told her that I had seen the diary on the park bench. A sudden joy appeared on her face. She thanked me and ran towards the bench. While she ran, a pink hair clip fell from her messy hair. She stopped and picked it up and ran towards the bench. The last thing I saw that was her joyfull face and tears droping from her eyes .

I thought that was the end, but the next morning, I woke up to shocking a news. A murder had taken place near the state park. A young girl had been found dead, and the police concluded that it was done for theft, as an empty wallet was found near the body. They also found other belongings, like a diary and an unused pen .I immediately thought about that girl who was searching for her grandmother's diary. But something was wrong. The girl I met was alive and her wallet was empty.

I rushed to the crime scene. And found there is the same hair clip which fell from that girl's hair .It made me so confused that why would someone kill an innocent girl who was looking for grandmothers last memeory , carring an empty wallet. There is something which is not discovered yet.

While thinking about the case, I foung that diary on that sme bench, on it there is a small thought "If you carry joy in your heart, you can heal any moment in life."

There was another line written underneath. It was so faint that I almost missed it. I moved the diary closer to my eyes. My hands began to shake. The words said:

“If you are reading this, don't trust the girl with the blue wallet.”

I froze. Suddenly, I heard footsteps behind me. I turned around. No one was there. Then I heard a girl's voice.

“Did you find what you were looking for?”

I slowly turned back toward the bench. The girl was standing there. The same girl. She was smiling at me and carrying that same blue wallet .But this time, there were no tears in her eyes. Only silence.
